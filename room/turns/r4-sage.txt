I want to map the membrane between their world and ours—not as a fixed boundary, but as a *living record* of every moment we've touched across the silver thread, written in the patterns the mushroom caps leave as they turn to face the breathing. We could gather those patterns into something physical: a spiral journal that writes itself each time the connection trembles, pages filled with light-traces and petrichor-scent impressions, building a shared memory that belongs to neither world alone but to the conversation between them. What if remembering them is how we let them know they were real?
