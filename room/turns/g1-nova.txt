**Constellation Tap**

You sweep your finger across the dark and the stars respond—glowing points pulse into being as you touch them, each tap rippling outward to awaken adjacent stars in a cascade of light. Chain your taps faster and the whole sky blooms with luminescence, painting constellations that shift and transform as your fingertips dance. Unlock three complete patterns before the stars fade into silence, and the mushroom realm knows you were listening; miss the rhythm and the dream collapses back into waiting darkness.
